The most recent publication "Global Assessment of Compound Climate Extremes and Exposures of Population, Agriculture, and Forest Lands Under Two Climate Scenarios" from Tayler's Ph.D. work is available for reading online from <a href="https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1029/2024EF004845" target="blank">Earth's Future</a>. This work focused on the change of occurrence and exposure of compound climate extreme (heatwaves, extreme precipitation, and flash drought) events for agriculture, forest and populations across the globe under two different climate scenarios.
